## Director policy

LoRA affects future generations, not direct repair of a frozen audio recording. Possible use: recurring vocal/style/arrangement problems across multiple independent songs. An adapter may also introduce regressions, memorization, or loss of intended arrangement. Do not train on faulty A/B/C or treat ASR non-regression as a positive training label. Use only authorized music and voice recordings with known provenance, consent and rights. Compare held-out songs using fixed base, score, lyrics, seeds, generation settings, companion strength, and one adapter/strength change per comparison. Train/validation split by song/recording; budget GPU work, checkpoint counts and renders. Evaluate lyric disagreement, score fidelity, technical QA, separate musical-quality evidence. Loss or KL alone does not prove improvement. No automatic training, checkpoint install, downloading, dataset preparation or new renders.
